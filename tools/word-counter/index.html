How to Use

  1. Type or paste your text into the main text area.
  2. The counts update automatically as you type — no button needed.
  3. Review the breakdown: words, characters, characters without spaces, sentences and paragraphs.
  4. Use the Copy button to copy your text or the Clear button to start over.

Practical Examples

  • A 500-word blog post usually needs 35–45 sentences and 4–6 paragraphs.
  • Tweet-style copy has a 280-character limit; the character count shows exactly where you stand.
  • Paste a full article to see paragraph and sentence counts before you submit it anywhere.

Key Features

  • Live word, character, sentence and paragraph counting
  • Characters with and without spaces
  • Handles Unicode text, em-dashes, apostrophes and line breaks correctly
  • Copy and clear actions with one click
Privacy: Your text never leaves your browser. All counting happens locally on your device, and nothing you paste is stored or sent to a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter treat hyphenated words?

Words like 'well-known' are counted as a single word, which matches standard word count behaviour. Apostrophes inside words, such as 'don't', also count as one word.

Does it count emoji and symbols?

Yes. Emoji and symbols count toward the character total. They are not counted as words unless part of a word-like sequence.

Is my text saved anywhere?

No. Everything is processed locally in your browser. Refreshing the page clears your text.
