Frequently Asked Questions

Does every browser support WebP?

WebP is supported by all modern browsers. We also detect encoding support in the browser you are using and warn you if it is not available.

Will my transparent PNG stay transparent?

Yes. WebP supports transparency, so transparent areas are preserved correctly.

Is WebP always smaller than PNG?

Usually, especially for photos and complex illustrations. For simple graphics with few colours, the difference can be small. We show the real comparison.
