Frequently Asked Questions

What BMI formula is used?

Metric: weight (kg) divided by height (m) squared. Imperial: 703 × weight (lb) / height (in) squared. Both produce the same BMI value.

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?

No. BMI is a general screening measure. It does not account for muscle mass, bone density, age, sex or distribution of fat. Always consult a healthcare professional for health decisions.

What are the standard categories?

Underweight below 18.5, normal 18.5–24.9, overweight 25–29.9, and obesity 30 and above. Ranges can vary slightly by organisation.
