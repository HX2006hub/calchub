Discount Calculator

Enter the original price and a discount percentage to see the final price, the exact amount you save, and what fraction of the price remains.

Discount CalculatorRuns in your browser

Enter a price and discount.

How to Use

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. The final price and savings update instantly.
  4. Optionally add tax if your price is pre-tax.

Practical Examples

  • A $120 jacket at 25% off costs $90 — you save $30.
  • A 10% discount on a $55 order saves $5.50.
  • Price with 7% sales tax added after a 15% discount.

Key Features

  • Final price after discount
  • Exact amount saved
  • Optional tax addition
  • Handles invalid or negative inputs gracefully
Privacy: All calculations happen locally in your browser. No values are stored or sent.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the discount calculated?

Discount amount = original price × discount % / 100. Final price = original − discount. If tax is enabled, we add the tax rate to the discounted price.

What if the discount is more than 100%?

We show the price going below zero to flag an invalid discount. Real discounts should be between 0% and 100%.

Does the calculator include sales tax?

Only if you enable the tax field. By default the result is pre-tax.
