Image Cropper

Crop an image to remove unwanted areas or match a specific aspect ratio. Preset ratios help you prepare images for social media, cards, avatars and print.

Image CropperRuns in your browser

Drop an image here or click to browse

Cropped locally — never uploaded

How to Use

  1. Upload or drag-and-drop an image.
  2. Drag the crop area to choose what to keep.
  3. Select an aspect ratio preset or leave it free.
  4. Download the cropped image.

Practical Examples

  • Crop a landscape photo to 1:1 for a profile picture.
  • Cut a photo to 16:9 for a YouTube thumbnail.
  • Remove extra background from a product screenshot.

Key Features

  • Drag-and-resize crop area
  • Common aspect ratio presets (1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 9:16)
  • Free-form cropping
  • Touch-friendly on mobile
  • Local processing with download
Privacy: Cropping happens locally in your browser. Your image is never uploaded, stored or transmitted.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change the crop area after selecting a preset?

Yes. You can drag the crop area around and adjust its edges within the preset ratio.

What happens to pixels outside the crop area?

They are removed. Only the area inside the crop box is kept in the exported image.

Does cropping reduce quality?

No. Cropping removes pixels but does not recompress the kept area, so quality is preserved.
