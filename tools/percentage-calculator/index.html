Percentage Calculator

Solve common percentage problems in seconds: find a percentage of a number, work out what percent one value is of another, or calculate percentage increase and decrease.

Enter values to calculate.

How to Use

  1. Choose the calculation mode: % of, is what % of, or increase/decrease.
  2. Enter the numbers for the mode you selected.
  3. The result updates instantly.
  4. Use the result in your work immediately — no signup needed.

Practical Examples

  • What is 15% of 240? Answer: 36.
  • You scored 42 out of 60 — that is 70%.
  • A price rises from 80 to 96 — that is a 20% increase.

Key Features

  • X% of Y calculation
  • X is what percent of Y
  • Percentage increase and decrease
  • Handles zero and invalid inputs gracefully
Privacy: Calculations run entirely in your browser. No values are sent to or stored on any server.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate X% of Y?

Multiply Y by X and divide by 100. For example, 15% of 240 = 240 × 15 / 100 = 36. The calculator does this instantly.

What does 'is what percent of' mean?

It tells you what percentage one number is of another. If 30 is what percent of 150, the answer is 20%.

Can it handle percentage decrease?

Yes. Select the increase/decrease mode and enter the original and new values. A negative result shows a decrease.
