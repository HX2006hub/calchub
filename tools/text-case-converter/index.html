Text Case Converter

Convert any text between UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case and alternating case. Useful for headlines, folder names, code and consistent communication.

How to Use

  1. Type or paste your text into the input area.
  2. Click a case option — Uppercase, Lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case or Alternate.
  3. The converted result appears instantly in the output box.
  4. Copy the result with one click.

Practical Examples

  • Turn a headline into Title Case: 'free tools for everyday tasks' becomes 'Free Tools for Everyday Tasks'.
  • Normalise pasted text to Sentence case for professional emails.
  • Quickly check how a style guide would capitalise a title.

Key Features

  • Uppercase, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case and alternating case
  • Correct handling of Unicode letters
  • Live conversion with no upload
  • One-click copy
Privacy: All conversion happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Title Case decided?

The converter capitalises the first letter of every word except short conjunctions, articles and prepositions (such as 'and', 'the' or 'of'), which stay lowercase unless they are the first word. Small differences exist between style guides; this is the most common convention.

Does it work with accented characters?

Yes. Unicode characters such as à, é, ü and their uppercase forms are handled correctly.

What is alternating case for?

Alternating case switches between upper and lower case (LiKe ThIs). It is sometimes used for mockery or emphasis in casual text. We convert it faithfully but keep it optional.
