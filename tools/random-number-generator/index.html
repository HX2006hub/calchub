Random Number Generator

Generate one or many random numbers inside any range you set. Choose whether numbers can repeat and whether you want integers only or decimals.

How to Use

  1. Enter a minimum and maximum value.
  2. Set how many numbers you need.
  3. Choose whether repeats are allowed.
  4. Click Generate and copy the result.

Practical Examples

  • Pick a number between 1 and 100 for a classroom raffle.
  • Generate 10 unique integers between 1 and 50 for a contest.
  • Roll a single random decimal between 0 and 1 for a decision.

Key Features

  • Custom minimum and maximum
  • Multiple numbers at once
  • Unique or repeatable results
  • Integers or decimals
Privacy: Numbers are generated locally in your browser. No input is stored or sent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the randomness fair?

We use a cryptographically secure random source where available, falling back to a tested seeded generator. The distribution is uniform across the valid range.

Can I get unique numbers only?

Yes. Toggle 'Allow repeats' off. If the range is too small for the requested count, we warn you instead of looping forever.

Does it support negative ranges?

Yes. Minimum and maximum can be negative, so ranges like −10 to 10 work fine.
