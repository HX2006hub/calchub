JPG to PNG Converter

Convert a JPG image to PNG format. PNG gives you lossless quality and is a common requirement for logos, screenshots and transparency workflows — though JPG itself does not contain transparency.

JPG has no alpha channel. If the source has transparent areas, they will appear white in the PNG.

  • Convert a JPG to PNG before adding it to a document that requires PNG.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my JPG have a white background after conversion?

JPG files have no transparency channel — every pixel is opaque. When we convert to PNG, any area that was white stays white. We do not fake transparency that did not exist.

Will the PNG be bigger than the JPG?

Usually yes. PNG is lossless and often larger than JPG at the same resolution. That is expected when you choose PNG.

Is quality lost during conversion?

No. PNG encodes the pixels losslessly, so the image content from the original JPG is preserved exactly.
