Duplicate Line Remover

Remove duplicate lines from any list while keeping the order of first appearance. Ideal for cleaning up CSV exports, keyword lists, emails and inventory data.

Ready — paste lines to see unique lines.

How to Use

  1. Paste your list into the input area — one item per line.
  2. Choose options: ignore case and trim whitespace.
  3. Click Remove Duplicates.
  4. Copy the cleaned list from the output box.

Practical Examples

  • An email list with repeated addresses is deduplicated before an import.
  • A keyword export with mixed-case entries is normalised with 'ignore case' enabled.
  • Product SKUs pasted from multiple sources are merged into one clean list.

Key Features

  • Removes duplicate lines and keeps first occurrence order
  • Optional case-insensitive matching
  • Optional whitespace trimming before comparison
  • Blank lines are removed automatically
Privacy: Your list is processed entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded or stored.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will it change the order of my lines?

No. The first occurrence of each line stays in place, and later duplicates are removed. The original relative order of unique lines is preserved.

What does 'ignore case' do?

When enabled, lines that differ only by letter case are treated as duplicates (for example 'Apple' and 'apple').

Are blank lines removed?

Yes. Empty lines are always removed unless every line is blank.
