How to Use

  1. Type or paste your text in the input box.
  2. The live count shows total characters and characters without spaces.
  3. Check the breakdown for the number of spaces, lines and words.
  4. Copy the result or the text itself with the buttons provided.

Practical Examples

  • A good meta description is typically 150–160 characters to avoid truncation in search results.
  • X posts have a 280-character limit (formerly Twitter); verify before you publish.
  • Product listing fields often cap at 100–200 characters — check before submitting.

Key Features

  • Live character counting with and without spaces
  • Counts spaces, lines and words too
  • Works with Unicode, emoji and special characters
  • Copy and clear buttons
Privacy: All counting happens locally in your browser. Your text is never uploaded, stored or shared.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do spaces count as characters?

Yes. The total character count includes spaces. We also show a separate count excluding spaces so you can use whichever your limit requires.

Are line breaks counted?

Line breaks count as one character each in the total. They are also shown in the lines count.

What counts as a character?

Every symbol in your text: letters, numbers, punctuation, spaces, emoji and line breaks each count as one character.
