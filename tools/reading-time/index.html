How to Use

  1. Paste your text into the box, or use the word count field directly.
  2. Choose a reading speed in words per minute (WPM), or keep the 225 WPM default.
  3. See the estimated reading time in minutes and seconds.
  4. Use the settings to fine-tune for technical content or young readers.

Practical Examples

  • A 1,100-word blog post at 225 WPM takes about 4 minutes 55 seconds to read.
  • Academic articles are often read slower, around 175 WPM; switch the speed to match.
  • If your landing page reads in under 60 seconds, most visitors will finish it.

Key Features

  • Reading time estimate based on word count
  • Adjustable WPM (words per minute)
  • Results in minutes and seconds
  • Estimates are clearly labelled as estimates
Privacy: Your text is processed entirely in your browser. We do not store or transmit any content you paste.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good default reading speed?

225 words per minute is a common average for adult English readers. Technical or dense content is often read around 175–200 WPM.

Is reading time exact?

No. Reading speed varies by person and content type, so the result is an estimate — not a guarantee.

Can I calculate without pasting text?

Yes. If you already know the word count, clear the text area and enter it in the word count field instead.
