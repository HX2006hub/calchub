Time Zone Converter

Convert a local time to any IANA time zone, with daylight saving handled correctly using the browser's Intl API. Perfect for scheduling calls across countries.

Time Zone ConverterRuns in your browser
—
Pick zones and a time.

Daylight saving is handled with the browser's IANA time zone data — not a fixed offset.

How to Use

  1. Pick the source time zone (or use your local one).
  2. Enter the date and time to convert.
  3. Pick the target time zone.
  4. Read the converted time, including the correct date.

Practical Examples

  • Schedule a 2 PM London call for a colleague in New York.
  • Convert an event time from UTC to your local zone.
  • Check whether a meeting falls on the same day in both zones.

Key Features

  • Full IANA time zone list
  • DST-aware conversion via Intl API
  • Shows both date and time in the result
  • Handles cross-day conversions
Privacy: Conversion runs locally using your browser's time zone data. Nothing is stored or sent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are daylight saving changes handled?

Yes. We use the Internationalization API with IANA time zones, so the result reflects the DST rules in effect at the selected date, not a fixed offset.

Why do some zones show different dates?

Zones east of UTC can be a day ahead and zones west can be a day behind. We always show the calendar date alongside the time.

Which time zones are available?

Every IANA zone the browser supports, which includes all major city zones, UTC variants and regions like Europe, America, Asia and Australia.
