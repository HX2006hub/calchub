Contact Us

Send feedback, a correction or a bug report about a CalcHub tool.

Last updated: November 2026

Get in touch

If a tool gives a result you did not expect, a page is unclear, or you have a suggestion, we would like to hear about it.

CalcHub does not publish a contact email address, because no monitored inbox has been confirmed. If you are reading this on a hosted preview or in the project repository, use that platform’s issue tracker or feedback channel to reach the maintainers.

Please do not send passwords, personal data or confidential material through any channel.

What to include

  • Which tool or page you are writing about.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Your browser and device, if the problem is visual or interactive.
  • For a calculation, the numbers you entered and the result you saw.

Response times

CalcHub is a small independent project with no formal support desk. We read feedback, but we cannot guarantee a reply to every message or a fix on any particular timeline.
