Contact Us

Contact the CalcHub team with feedback, bug reports or suggestions.

Last updated: November 2026

Get in touch

We welcome feedback, bug reports and tool suggestions.

This site is maintained by a small independent team. We do not publish a contact address here because no monitored inbox has been confirmed yet. If you are viewing this site through a hosted preview or repository, use that platform’s issue tracker or feedback channel to reach the maintainers.

Please do not send sensitive personal data through any channel.

What to include

  • Which tool or page you are writing about.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened.
  • Your browser and device (if relevant).

Response times

This is a small independent project. We cannot guarantee a response to every message, but we read everything and prioritise issues that affect many users.
