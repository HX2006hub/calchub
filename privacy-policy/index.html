Privacy Policy

How CalcHub handles your information: tools run in your browser, no accounts, no analytics and a clear note on local storage.

Last updated: November 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what happens to information when you use CalcHub. The short version: the tools process your input in your browser, and we do not collect it.

Information we collect

We operate a static website with no user accounts and no database of visitors. You do not need to provide a name, email address or any other personal details to use a tool.

CalcHub itself does not collect or store visitor information. The company hosting these pages may record standard technical data such as IP address and browser type while delivering them; that is handled under the host’s own policy, not by CalcHub.

Cookies and local storage

We do not set cookies and we do not run advertising. The only browser storage used is localStorage in the two counter tools, and only if you tick the optional "Keep count after refresh" checkbox. That number stays on your device; unticking the box or clearing site data removes it.

Analytics

We do not use Google Analytics, Cloudflare Analytics or any other analytics service. We do not run advertising pixels or trackers, and we never log the content you type into a tool.

Local processing of tool data

All tool processing happens in your browser using standard Web APIs such as Canvas, File, Blob, Clipboard, Web Crypto and Intl. Your text, uploaded images and calculated values are not uploaded to our server, are not stored by us, and disappear when you close the page.

Third-party services

We do not load third-party fonts, analytics or advertising scripts. The only bundled third-party code is the open-source QR code generator library, which is served from this site itself and runs locally in your browser. Your hosting provider delivers the static pages under its own privacy policy.

Data security

Because we do not collect tool inputs or account data, there is little personal data for us to protect. The pages are served over HTTPS by the hosting provider.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since we do not collect tool inputs or account data, there is generally nothing to access or delete. To raise a privacy question, use the Contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or the law changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.
