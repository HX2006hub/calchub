Privacy Policy

How CalcHub protects your privacy: tools run in your browser, no accounts, minimal data collection and clear disclosure of cookies and analytics.

Last updated: November 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CalcHub handles information when you visit and use our website. We designed the site to minimise data collection: the tools themselves process your content in your browser and do not send it to our servers.

Information we collect

We operate a static website with no user accounts and no database of visitors. We do not require you to provide a name, email address or any personal details to use any tool.

Depending on how the site is hosted and connected to third-party services, standard technical data may be collected automatically — for example your IP address, browser type, referring page, and pages visited. This data is used by hosting providers and analytics services to operate and improve the site, and is generally retained according to those providers’ policies.

Cookies

We do not set advertising cookies ourselves. Some tools use your browser’s local storage only if you explicitly enable a "keep count" option. That data stays on your device.

If we later enable Google AdSense, Google may use cookies (including DoubleClick cookies) to serve and personalise ads. We would clearly update this policy, and where required, ask for consent before personalised advertising. You can control or disable cookies in your browser settings.

Analytics

If the site uses an analytics service such as Google Analytics or privacy-friendly alternatives (for example Cloudflare Web Analytics), it may process aggregate, privacy-preserving data about visits. We do not combine this with personal identifiers, and we do not log the content you type into tools.

Local processing of tool data

All tool processing happens in your browser using standard Web APIs such as Canvas, File, Blob, Clipboard, Web Crypto and Intl. Your text, uploaded images and calculated values are not uploaded to our server, are not stored by us, and disappear when you close the page.

Third-party services

Our pages may load resources from third-party providers for core functions (such as hosting, fonts or analytics). These providers process only the minimal technical data needed to deliver those resources, under their own privacy policies.

Data security

Because we do not collect or store tool inputs, there is little personal data for us to protect. Standard security measures (HTTPS, updated dependencies) are used to keep the site itself safe.

Your rights

Depending on your location (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since we do not collect tool inputs or account data, there is generally nothing to access or delete. To raise a privacy question, contact us via the Contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or law changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.
