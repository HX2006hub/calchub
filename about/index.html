About Us

What CalcHub is, what the 30 tools cover and how they handle your input.

Last updated: November 2026

What CalcHub is

CalcHub is a collection of 30 free tools for small, recurring jobs: counting words, checking a percentage, resizing an image, generating a password, or finding the time in another city. Each page contains one tool and a short explanation of how to use it.

There is no account to create and nothing to install. You open a tool, put in your text, numbers or file, and read the result.

What you will find

The tools are grouped into six collections:

  • Text Tools — word and character counts, reading time, case conversion, duplicate-line removal and placeholder text.
  • Image & File Tools — compress, resize, crop, convert formats and generate a favicon.
  • Calculators — percentages, age, date difference, unit conversion, BMI and discounts.
  • Generators & Converters — passwords, QR codes, UUIDs, random values, name picking and JSON tools.
  • Time & Productivity — countdown, stopwatch, Pomodoro timer and time zone conversion.
  • Counters & Trackers — two simple manual tallies.

How the tools work

Processing happens in your browser. Text you paste, images you drop and numbers you enter are handled on your device using standard browser features, and are not sent to a server. Where a result depends on an assumption — an average reading speed, a standard BMI range, an average month length — the tool page says so.

The explanations on each page are written against what the tool actually does. If a timer has no sound, or a converter always outputs a particular format, that is stated rather than left for you to discover.

Privacy

Because the tools process input locally, we do not receive what you enter. The only optional storage is in the counter tools, where a tickbox can keep a running number in your own browser. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
